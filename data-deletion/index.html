Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 30 August 2026 · See also Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

AI Media Factory (“the App”) is a private, single-operator tool that connects only to social-media accounts owned by its Operator. Because the App holds data only about the Operator’s own connected accounts, deletion is immediate and complete on request:

  • Disconnecting an account in the App revokes and destroys its stored OAuth tokens (the sealed token material is overwritten).
  • You may also revoke the App’s access at any time from the platform side: Google account permissions, TikTok app settings → Security → Apps and websites, or Instagram settings → Website permissions.
  • To request deletion of any and all data associated with a connected account, email aimediafactory.ops@gmail.com with the account name. Requests are honoured within 30 days; token material is destroyed immediately.
