AI Media Factory

Original worlds, produced end to end.

AI Media Factory is an independent studio platform for building original media brands: concepts are planned from each brand’s creative DNA, generated with modern AI tools, reviewed by a human, and published on a schedule — with budgets, rights records, and AI disclosure built into every step.

01Plan

Each brand has a defined voice, palette, and topic range. A planning engine proposes new, non-repeating concepts and episodic series.

02Generate

Original stills, motion, and thumbnails are generated per concept — no real people, no franchises, no recycled uploads.

03Review

A human quality gate checks every piece before anything is scheduled, and hard spending caps govern all generation.

04Publish

Uploads go out privately first, then are scheduled through each platform’s official API with full metadata and disclosure.
